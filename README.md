# Oxford Neurotech Hackathon 2026 (team Avengers, AI stream)

Work in progress, 7-8 November 2026.

## Stream rules we must meet (AI / neural decoding, sponsored by Tobii)
- AI must be central: we train, fine-tune or meaningfully adapt a model.
- Neural or physiological data is a core input (live or public dataset).
- Evaluation: held-out split, a baseline, and at least one metric.
- No covert recording; no inferring health conditions or other protected traits from people at the event.
- All code written during the hackathon; credit libraries, datasets and pre-trained models.
