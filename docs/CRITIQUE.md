### 1. [critical] fatigue-1: Flat or railed baseline makes ASR zero every later window; zeros pass the clean gate and drive fatigue to 0 'Recovering' instead of 'Held'
- Where: `loadmonitor/load_gauge.py` 400, 414, 261
- Claim: The clean gate (line 400) accepts any channel whose post-chain RMS is under 100 uV and has no lower limit. If the 30 s baseline is taken while channels are flat or railed, the ASR reference std is about 0. Every later window then has all of its components dropped and comes out as exact zeros. The zeros count as clean, so fat_now = log10(1e-12) = -12 (line 414) and fatigue falls to 0. fatigue_held (line 261) is clean_n < 3, which is false here, so the page shows 'Recovering' and never 'Held · noisy'. gauge_server's share_seconds_3plus_clean also counts these channels, so the Claude debrief is told the signal was good.
- Evidence: Offline emulation of the gauge path (/tmp/critic7/fat_emul.py, dbg2.py), checked against the replay logs in /tmp/replay_*.csv. 222646: baseline raw std was 0 uV on all 5 model channels and the ASR ref std was [0 0 0 0 0]. All 164 of 164 post-baseline windows are all-zero after the chain, 137 give a ratio of exactly -12, and the replay log has 136 of 164 rows below 40, ending at 0.0. 210947: after a dead baseline the channels rail (raw std 86,000 to 151,000 uV), yet they are zeroed and counted clean. 74 of 147 rows are below 40 and the 'physical' segment reads 0.0. 211346: 79 of 93 rows below 40. Three of the 16 replayed recordings are affected.
- Adversary: Checked the code: the ASR ref is std+1e-9 (model_search.py:97), so after a flat baseline every component fails the 5x test (:103) and the window rebuilds as exact zeros. The clean gate at load_gauge.py:400 has no lower bound, and fatigue_held at :261 is clean_n<3, so it stays false. My own emulation (/tmp/adv7/emu.py) found raw std 0 on all 5 model channels through the baseline windows in 210947, 211346 and 222646 (and in 210731, which is not replayed), with ASR ref [1e-9 x5]. Their first 60 monitoring windows give a ratio of -12.000, while the same windows without ASR give 0.965 (222646) and 3.20 (210947). The replay row counts match (222646: 136 of 164 rows below 40, ending at 0.0). It is worse than stated: in 222646 load is 0.0 on all 164 rows and every feature logs 0.000, so the whole page reads 'Calm 0' and 'Recovering 0' with no Held flag. The root cause is upstream (see missed issue 1).
- Fix: A channel counts as clean only if its post-chain RMS is over 1 uV, its raw window has no samples at +/-200000 and its raw std is under a ceiling such as 2000 uV. Reject fat_now unless all three median envelopes are above about 1e-3 uV and the log ratio is within -1 to 3. Do not fit ASR or finish the baseline when any component's ref std is below 1e-3 or most baseline windows have fewer than 3 clean channels. Recompute base_fat with the post-ASR chain and the same gate that monitoring uses.

### 2. [high] alpha-adv1: Alpha check passes with NO alpha when eye closure changes the 1/f fit bands (muscle relax or blinks stop)
- Where: `loadmonitor/load_gauge.py` 55 to 63 (fit bands), 379 (rule)
- Claim: alpha_height measures 8 to 13 Hz against a line fitted on 3 to 7 Hz and 15 to 30 Hz. Any fall in power in those fit bands at eye closure lowers the line and raises the 'alpha height' even with no alpha present. Eye closure plausibly does exactly this: blinks and saccades stop (less below 7 Hz), and face and neck relax (less EMG above 15 Hz). His posterior spectrum already carries high-frequency excess (OVERNIGHT.md 34: control band +0.6 to +2.0 dB, above the alpha band). The null test (eyes never closed) cannot see this. The result would be a false 'Brain signal confirmed', the one error the check exists to prevent.
- Evidence: Scratchpad a5/b.py, 531 check positions (5 s step) on his 19 raw files, NO alpha added, closed phase only spectrally reshaped. Cutting power above 15 Hz in the closed phase by 3 dB gives PASS 6.4 percent, by 6 dB 28.8 percent, by 10 dB 74.8 percent. Cutting below 7 Hz by 6 dB gives 18.5 percent. Cutting both by 6 dB gives 85.1 percent. The unmodified null is 0.94 percent. The height shifts about 0.39 dB per dB of power lost above 15 Hz and about 0.34 dB per dB lost below 7 Hz.
- Fix: Make the rule band-specific. Compute absolute closed/open power ratios for 8 to 13 Hz, 3 to 7 Hz and 15 to 30 Hz per channel. PASS only if the 8 to 13 Hz ratio is >= +3 dB and beats both flanking-band ratios by >= 3 dB, or require a local maximum between 8 and 12 Hz in the closed-minus-open log spectrum. Print the flank changes in the result text. Add a test to tests/ that replays the closed phase with the flanks attenuated 6 dB and no alpha, and asserts FAIL.
