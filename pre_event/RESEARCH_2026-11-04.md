## SwallowCue key evidence (checked 4 Nov)
- Drooling affects up to 70% of people with PD; the cause is too few swallows, not excess saliva: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9955579/
- McNaney 2019 PD-CUE: wristband buzzing ONCE A MINUTE, 28 people, self-reported improvement: https://nrl.northumbria.ac.uk/id/eprint/39104
- CueBand (Northumbria, Parkinson's UK funded) trial with 300 people, FIXED SCHEDULE cueing, no swallow sensing: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN59284050 and protocol https://doaj.org/article/407d3361e66a4d649eb645c7d7175266
- CLOSEST COMPETITOR: Northwestern "Aria" patch, NCT04664634, senses swallows and gives haptic cues, recruiting about 60, planned end May 2026, no results published; swallows labelled from sensor AUDIO (likely acoustic sensing): https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04664634
- Offline under-chin EMG swallow detection in PD, 83% accuracy: https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/25/22/6834
- No hackathon project found.
